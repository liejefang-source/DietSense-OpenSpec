\begin{abstract}
DietSense uses dialogue to estimate nutrients in Taiwanese meals. We plan to compare its accuracy and usability with existing methods.\end{abstract}
\section{Introduction}
A diner can describe a lunch box in one sentence. Even when the dish name is clear, rice weight and added oil may remain unknown. We propose an interface that asks about missing information and produces a meal summary.
\section{Related Work}
Food tables supply reference values and language models can interpret descriptions. These tools motivate combining dialogue and lookup.
